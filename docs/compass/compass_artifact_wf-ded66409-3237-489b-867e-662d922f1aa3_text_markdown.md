# The Blais Family: A Genealogical and Business Intelligence Dossier

## Executive Summary

This investigation reveals a French-Canadian American family deeply rooted in motorsports across three generations, culminating in the establishment of Integrated Engineering, a successful automotive performance company. **Despite exhaustive research across genealogical databases, racing archives, public records, and historical newspapers, the father's full identity remains unverified, with only one unconfirmed lead**. The family maintained remarkable privacy while building a multi-million dollar engineering enterprise from garage operations in Salt Lake City.

### The $7,500 Turbo Kit - Strategic Pivot Analysis

**Product Details**:
- High-end turbo upgrade requiring custom air intake development
- OEM intake components incompatible with turbo installation
- Targeted serious enthusiasts and racers

**Discontinuation Rationale** (Peter Blais, CompanyWeek interview):
"We built this $7,500 turbo upgrade and as part of that, we had to make an air intake, because none of the OEM stuff would fit and connect to it. Now we sell thousands and thousands of those [air intakes] a year, but the turbo kit's long gone."

**Strategic Insight**:
- Price point ($7,500) limited market to extreme enthusiasts
- Labor-intensive installation reduced addressable market
- Air intake component became best-selling product at fraction of cost
- **Key Lesson**: Entry-level modifications bring customers into brand ecosystem, creating pathway to hardcore components "years down the road"
- Validated business model shift from niche extreme products to accessible performance upgrades

**Current Turbo Offerings**:
- K04 turbo kits (~$2,000-3,000 range)
- IE450T big turbo kit for MK6 Golf R (500HP+, complete system)
- More accessible pricing aligned with mainstream market
