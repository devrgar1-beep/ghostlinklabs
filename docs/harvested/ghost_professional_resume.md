## CASE FILES

**Intermittent radio failure.** Only when it rains. Five techs looked at it. Replaced radio twice. Replaced antennas. Still failed.

**Solution:** Voltage drop on ground circuit. Corroded connector behind dash. Moisture got in. Found it with a scope and spray bottle. Fixed with a $2 connector and dielectric grease.

Diagnostic time: 6 hours. Parts cost: $2. Value of not having a cop with no radio: Everything.

---

**Light bar blowing fuses.** Voltage good. Current draw in spec. Wiring perfect. Fuse size correct. Still popped.

**Solution:** Inrush current. LEDs pull 3-4x steady-state current for 50 milliseconds on startup. Too fast for basic meter. Too much for fuse.

Fixed with soft-start relay. Gradually ramps voltage over 200ms. Problem solved.

---

**Entire vehicle flipping out randomly.** Radio garbled. Lights flickering. Computer rebooting. Completely unpredictable.

**Solution:** High-power radar unit mounted next to vehicle computer. No shielding. Radar pulses were electromagnetic shotgun blasts into the computer.

Moved radar. Added shielding. Rerouted cables. Installed ferrite cores. Done.
