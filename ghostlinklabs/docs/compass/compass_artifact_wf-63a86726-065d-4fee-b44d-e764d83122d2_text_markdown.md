### Intensive application process (major time investment)

**Stage 1**: Online application with qualification questions

**Stage 2**: **CCAT (Criteria Cognitive Aptitude Test)**
- 50 multiple choice questions in 15 minutes (~18 seconds per question)
- Must score **85-90th percentile** (approximately 40+ correct answers)
- Covers verbal, math, logic, spatial reasoning
- Score carries over if you reapply; must wait 6 months if you fail

**Stage 3**: Language proficiency test (2-minute English audio recording)

**Stage 4**: Technical assessments
- HackerRank-style challenges (40 minutes to 3 hours)
- Real-world problem-solving with access to LLMs (encouraged to use AI)
- Fixed LLM message quota to solve problems
- Free response questions on practical scenarios

**Stage 5**: Three 30-minute interviews
- VP of Engineering → SVP of Engineering/Operations → CEO
- Focus on thought processes, decision-making, assessment approach
- NOT focused on technical proficiency (proven via assessments)

**Stage 6**: **Proctored CCAT (PCCAT)** - Major red flag
- Must retake CCAT with webcam proctor watching
- Must join from phone as well for dual camera view
- Reportedly harder than initial CCAT
- **Must achieve same or higher score or you're disqualified**
- If you fail after completing entire process, you don't get paid for work already done

**Stage 7**: RemoteU training week
- Reading tasks, Excel assignments, internal tools training
- Metrics tracked and ranked against cohort
- Presentation required at end; VP decides continuation
- Over 50% of some cohorts fail various stages

**Stage 8** (some roles): RemoteCamp - 3 weeks intensive
- 100+ test cases assigned
- Daily metrics tracking and forms
- Reports of 50-60+ hour weeks (6:30am-8pm)
- High dropout rate by design

**Timeline**: Can take 1 day (hiring tournament) to several weeks (standard process)

- Must be constantly "active" on monitoring software

**Key performance metric:**

- Measures "how much of your work did not need to be redone"
- Tracked daily with required explanations for failures

- "Ridiculously high targets" reported by employees
