\begin{abstract}
We present a distributed multi-agent system that treats models as composable residues that fold into task-specific configurations. A shaped energy over configurations steers motif assembly; results show gains over strong single-model baselines with an IIT-inspired integration proxy. Safety and governance mechanisms are included.
\end{abstract}
\section{Introduction}
GhostLink frames distributed reasoning as bio-inspired folding. We sketch the folding energy, motif library, and empirical benefits.
\section{Related Work}
Protein-inspired computation; multi-agent orchestration; integration measures; consensus in distributed systems.
\section{Method}
Configuration $C$ is a directed multigraph; minimize $\mathcal{E}(C)$ combining MI-based coupling, redundancy penalty, latency pressure, and complexity. Motif-preserving moves with annealing under load.
\section{Architecture}
64 agents on FCC lattice; $\alpha$ recursion and $\beta$ parallelism; chaperone directors; stigmergic traces; PBFT with batching/pipelining; edge-first infra.
\section{Experiments}
Mixed workloads (math, coding, QA, planning); report accuracy, latency, cost, hallucination, uptime. Ablations remove motifs/chaperones/PBFT; topology swap; sequence shuffle.

Graph-level proxy measures information loss under minimum partition; not canonical $\Phi$.

Fitness gates, kill-switch; immutable audit; least-privilege scopes; incident playbooks.

Bio-inspired folding is a practical engineering lens for distributed intelligence.
