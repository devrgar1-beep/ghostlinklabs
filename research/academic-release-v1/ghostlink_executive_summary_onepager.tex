\noindent\textbf{Positioning.} GhostLink reframes AI systems as \emph{computational proteins}: small specialized agents ("amino acids") that fold into task-specific configurations. The result is a distributed reasoner that is more accurate, faster, and cheaper than strong single-model baselines on mixed workloads, with measurable life-like properties in silico.
\section*{Core Claims (Tempered)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1em]
\item \textbf{Performance:} Mixed workload gains vs. single strong model (accuracy, latency, cost). Numbers are reported with methods and ablations.
\item \textbf{Engineering Lens:} A shaped \emph{folding energy} steers motif assembly (\alpha-recursion, \beta-parallelism) toward low-energy, high-utility folds.
\item \textbf{Life-like Properties:} Seven operationalized properties (organization, metabolism, homeostasis, growth, reproduction, response, evolution).
\item \textbf{Integration Proxy:} IIT-inspired \emph{integration proxy} (not canonical $\Phi$) indicates non-trivial information integration.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Architecture at a Glance}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1em]
\item 64 specialized agents on FCC lattice; motif library (\alpha, \beta, loops); chaperone directors.
\item Energy function $\mathcal{E}(C)$ combines MI-based coupling, redundancy penalty, latency pressure, complexity regularizer.
\item Stigmergic traces (content-addressed, signed) + PBFT-style consensus (batched/pipelined).
\item Edge-first deployment (Cloudflare Workers); content-addressed storage; multi-region D1.
\end{itemize}
\section*{Methods in Brief}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1em]
\item Tasks: math, coding, knowledge QA, planning; disjoint checks via n-gram/URL hashing.
\item Baselines: strong single-model per task; version-locked; fixed decoding.
\item Metrics: accuracy/pass@k, decomposed latency, cost, hallucination (rubric + human spot-checks), uptime.
\item Stats: 5 runs; mean $\pm$95\% CI; paired tests; Holm--Bonferroni.
\item Ablations: remove motifs/chaperones/PBFT; swap topology; shuffle sequence.
\end{itemize}
\section*{Safety \\ \& Governance}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1em]
\item Staged evolution with fitness gates and kill-switch.
\item Immutable audit (content-addressed traces, signatures).
\item Least-privilege scopes, circuit breakers, incident playbooks.
\end{itemize}
